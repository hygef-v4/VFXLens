# Dreamy / Atmospheric

**Defining Characteristics:** Soft lighting, atmospheric haze, depth separation, and gentle motion pacing that create an airy mood. Heavy glow or full-frame blur is not mandatory.

## Proposed Assembly Workflow

1. Select a shot with a clear subject and distinct highlight regions.
2. Control [glow, color, and bokeh](../techniques/lighting-and-color/glow-color-bokeh.md) as separate, deliberate stages.
3. Layer in [light leaks, fog, or particles](../resources/overlays.md) aligned with the light source and composition.
4. When compositing alpha objects, preserve silhouette and color balance, adding depth occlusion or subtle drift matched to the shot.
5. Check facial features, eyes, typography, highlights, and shadow detail; balance SFX/ambience in the audio mix.

## Common Pitfalls

Fog washing out black levels, glow blowing out detail, bokeh obscuring faces, alpha cutouts looking overly sharp against a soft background, or layers drifting out of rhythm. Solo/toggle individual layers to evaluate their actual contribution and remove redundant layers when the shot already communicates the mood.

**Case Studies:** [White Ferrari](../case-studies/white-ferrari.md) documents overlay/blur stacking; [Takt Op](../case-studies/takt-op.md) includes glow keyframe examples where certain keys fall outside the layer's `in`/`out` range. Neither case study classifies the entire source video under a single style.
